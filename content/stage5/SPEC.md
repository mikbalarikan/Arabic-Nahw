# Stage 5 · Nouns in rafʿ (مَرْفُوعَاتُ الأَسْمَاءِ)

Read /home/claude/arabic-nahw/content/STAGES_5_8_SPEC.md first (shared rules, canonical options). This file adds the Stage 5 plan.

Goal: the seven jobs that put a noun in rafʿ, and the four signs of rafʿ for every noun group.
Signs of rafʿ: damma (singular, broken plural, sound fem. plural; diptotes too, with one damma and no tanween),
alif (dual), wāw (sound masc. plural, five nouns), hidden damma (maqṣūr, manqūṣ, noun + ـي "my").
A fixed word in a rafʿ job is "in the place of rafʿ" (Stage 3).

Scope notes:
- No present verbs. Verbs: past (active and passive) and order verbs.
- Allowed "place of rafʿ" fixed words: pronouns هُوَ/هُمْ..., pointing words هَذَا، relative الَّذِي; هَذَانِ / اللَّذَانِ are
  muʿrab (alif in rafʿ): fine as a stretch item with a clear why.
- Inna's noun and kāna's information appear in naṣb; Stage 7 drills them. Here only ask their case (naṣb) if needed.
