# Stage 7 · Nouns in naṣb (مَنْصُوبَاتُ الأَسْمَاءِ)

Read /home/claude/arabic-nahw/content/STAGES_5_8_SPEC.md first (shared rules, canonical options). This file adds the Stage 7 plan.

Goal: the jobs that put a noun in naṣb and the signs of naṣb for every group:
fatha (singular, broken plural, diptote; manqūṣ: the fatha SHOWS on the ي: رَأَيْتُ القَاضِيَ، قَاضِيًا), alif (five nouns: أَبَاكَ),
yāʾ (dual ـَيْنِ, sound masc. plural ـِينَ), kasra in place of fatha (sound fem. plural: رَأَيْتُ الطَّالِبَاتِ، مُسْلِمَاتٍ),
hidden fatha (maqṣūr: رَأَيْتُ مُوسَى / الفَتَى; noun + ـي "my": رَأَيْتُ أَبِي). Stage 2 taught naṣb by fatha for object, purpose,
time/place and state: Stage 7 deepens these with all groups and adds the remaining manṣūbāt.
