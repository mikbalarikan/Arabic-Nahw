# Stage 6 · Nouns in jarr (مَجْرُورَاتُ الأَسْمَاءِ)

Read /home/claude/arabic-nahw/content/STAGES_5_8_SPEC.md first (shared rules, canonical options). This file adds the Stage 6 plan.

Goal: the three ways a noun is in jarr (after a preposition, as owner in iḍāfa, as a follower) and the signs of jarr:
kasra (singular, broken plural, sound fem. plural ـَاتٍ / ـَاتِ), yāʾ (dual ـَيْنِ, sound masc. plural ـِينَ, five nouns أَبِي...),
fatha in place of kasra for a diptote with no ال and not owned (مَرَرْتُ بِمَسَاجِدَ، سَلَّمْتُ عَلَى أَحْمَدَ), and hidden kasra
(maqṣūr, manqūṣ with ال or owned: فِي النَّادِي; manqūṣ without ال: مَرَرْتُ بِقَاضٍ, noun + ـي "my"). Stage 5 (rafʿ) is known.
