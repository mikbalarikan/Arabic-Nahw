## c01-title  (kind chapter; blocks [2])
   block 2 [p]: Arabic has a word for near things and a word for far things. Each one also changes for male, female and plural.

## c01-rule  (kind box; blocks [6])
   block 6 [rule]: Learn the last vowel too. هَذَا ends in alif, هَذِهِ and هَؤُلَاءِ in kasra, ذَلِكَ ، تِلْكَ ، أُولَئِكَ and هُنَاكَ in fatha. These endings never change.

   mark:6  هُنَاكَ
## c01-tip  (kind box; blocks [7])
   block 7 [tip]: Tip: هَؤُلَاءِ and أُولَئِكَ are for groups of people, male or female: هَؤُلَاءِ طُلَّابٌ ، هَؤُلَاءِ نِسَاءٌ . For plural things Arabic normally uses هَذِهِ : هَذِهِ كُتُبٌ "these are books".

## c03-cols  (kind cols; blocks [14])
   block 14 [cols]: No ال: a sentence هَذَا كِتَابٌ "This is a book." هَذَا is the Topic and كِتَابٌ is the Information. With ال: a phrase هَذَا الْكِتَابُ "this book". It is not finished yet. Add Information: هَذَا الْكِتَابُ جَدِيدٌ .

## c03-examples  (kind examples; blocks [15])
   block 15 [examples]: هَذَا بَيْتٌ. This is a house. A full sentence. هَذَا الْبَيْتُ قَرِيبٌ. This house is near. هَذَا الْبَيْتُ is the Topic part. قَرِيبٌ is the Information. تِلْكَ سَيَّارَةٌ. That is a car. A full sentence. تِلْكَ السَّيَّارَةُ جَدِيدَةٌ. That car is new. تِلْكَ السَّيَّارَةُ means "that car".

   block 17 [tip]: Tip: A noun in iḍāfa has no ال , so it makes a sentence: هَذَا بَيْتُ حَامِدٍ "This is Hamid's house." (Madinah lesson 5)

## c04-examples  (kind examples; blocks [20])
   block 20 [examples]: هَذَا قَلَمٌ وَهَذِهِ سَاعَةٌ. This is a pen and this is a watch. قَلَمٌ is masculine, سَاعَةٌ is feminine. ذَلِكَ مَسْجِدٌ وَتِلْكَ مَدْرَسَةٌ. That is a mosque and that is a school. Far: ذَلِكَ for masculine, تِلْكَ for feminine. هَؤُلَاءِ طُلَّابٌ وَأُولَئِكَ رِجَالٌ. These are students and those are men. Groups of people: هَؤُلَاءِ ، أُولَئِكَ . هَذِهِ بِنْتٌ. This is a girl. بِنْتٌ has no ة , but it is feminine. Use هَذِهِ .

## c05-title  (kind chapter; blocks [22])
   block 22 [p]: A pointing word is fixed, but it still has a job. The job gives it a place. The noun after it shows the ending that the pointing word cannot show.
## c05-examples  (kind examples; blocks [23])
   block 23 [examples]: هَذَا كِتَابٌ. This is a book. هَذَا is the Topic. Fixed, in the place of rafʿ. دَخَلَ هَذَا الرَّجُلُ الْمَسْجِدَ. This man entered the mosque. هَذَا is the doer. Place of rafʿ. See the damma on الرَّجُلُ . فَتَحَ الطَّالِبُ هَذَا الْبَابَ. The student opened this door. هَذَا is the object. Place of naṣb. See the fatha on الْبَابَ . فِي هَذَا الْبَيْتِ in this house هَذَا comes after a preposition. Place of jarr. See the kasra on الْبَيْتِ . كِتَابُ هَذَا الطَّالِبِ this student's book هَذَا is the owner. Place of jarr.

   mark:3  هَذَا
   mark:4  الرَّجُلُ
   mark:5  فَتَحَ الطَّالِبُ هَذَا الْبَابَ.

## c06-rule  (kind box; blocks [26])
   block 26 [rule]: The words for "these two" change. هَذَانِ (two males) and هَاتَانِ (two females) are flexible. In naṣb and jarr they become هَذَيْنِ and هَاتَيْنِ . You learn them with the dual in Stage 4.

## summary  (kind summary; blocks [28])
   block 28 [summary]: In short Near: هَذَا ، هَذِهِ ، هَؤُلَاءِ . Far: ذَلِكَ ، تِلْكَ ، أُولَئِكَ . Place: هُنَا ، هُنَاكَ . Choose by the noun: near or far, male or female, one or a group of people. هَذَا كِتَابٌ is a sentence. هَذَا الْكِتَابُ is a phrase: "this book". Pointing words are fixed, but they have a place: rafʿ, naṣb or jarr. هَذَانِ and هَاتَانِ are not fixed. They wait for Stage 4.
